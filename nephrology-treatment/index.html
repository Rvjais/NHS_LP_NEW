Who we are

This website is operated by Nasa & Hub Superspeciality Hospital, a multi-specialty hospital in Jalandhar, Punjab. You can reach us on 0181-4633333 (appointments), 0181-4707700 (24-hour emergency) or info@nhshospital.in.

What we collect

When you submit an appointment request we collect the name, mobile number, city, department or problem, and preferred date that you enter, together with your consent to be contacted. We do not ask for medical records, payment details or identity documents through this website.

Like most websites, ours records standard technical information (browser, device type, pages visited) through analytics and advertising tags, including Google Ads conversion tracking. These tags may set cookies in your browser.

Why we use it

Your enquiry details are used only to contact you about that enquiry — to confirm an appointment, tell you which reports to bring, or answer your question. Technical information is used to understand how the site is used and to measure our advertising.

Who sees it

Enquiry details are seen by our appointment desk and the relevant clinical department. We use a third-party form service to receive submissions and an analytics and advertising provider to measure site usage. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and to meet our record-keeping obligations, and are then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details, or ask us to stop contacting you, by writing to info@nhshospital.in or calling the appointment desk. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Children

This website is not directed at children. Appointment requests for a child should be submitted by a parent or guardian.

Medical information on this site

Pages on this website describe the services the hospital provides and give general health information. They are not medical advice and cannot be used to diagnose or treat a condition. Nothing you send through this website creates a doctor–patient relationship, and this website is never a route for emergency care.
